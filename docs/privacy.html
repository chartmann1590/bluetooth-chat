Privacy Policy

MeshTalk for Android · Hartmann Studios

Version notice: The descriptions of changed features below apply to MeshTalk for Google Play version 1.0.20261121.0939 and later versions with those features, when available. If you are still using an earlier release, these changes do not install or enable new features on your device. Read the policy previously shown for earlier releases. The direct-download edition may differ as noted below.

At a glance: MeshTalk sends chat, attachments and walkie-talkie audio over Bluetooth. Public posts are readable by other mesh participants; direct messages are encrypted for the intended recipient. Internet features such as private reports, maps, Play Billing, ads, optional diagnostics, model downloads and public feedback have separate data paths.

On your device and over the mesh

Your nickname, mesh identity keypair, peers, saved messages and attachments, block list and settings are kept in app storage. The keys used to sign and encrypt traffic stay in app storage; your public signing and agreement keys and nickname are announced to peers. Android backup is enabled. Your OS backup or transfer settings may copy eligible app data; consult your device’s backup settings.

If you send public text, images, files, location or voice, people on the mesh can see or hear it, and might retain, relay or copy it. Direct content is encrypted from the sender to the intended recipient; packets can be relayed and some routing metadata (such as public keys) is visible to relays. This description is about direct mesh content, not every data path in MeshTalk. MeshTalk does not provide centrally hosted chat history. Blocking on your device hides the peer on that device; it cannot erase content on another person’s device.

Nearby-device and Bluetooth permissions support mesh connections. Some older Android releases require location permission for discovery. Optional location sharing puts your selected coordinates in the message. In the Play edition, you can choose whether to load a Bluetooth Radar backdrop and whether to attach a map when sharing your location. If you choose a map and the tiles are not cached, the OpenStreetMap tile service receives HTTPS requests corresponding to the area (which can disclose a small area) and ordinary network information; sharing coordinates without a map does not request tiles. The direct edition may fetch the map automatically when you use those features. Voice recording uses the microphone when you record; chat may also use media you pick. Notifications, including direct and optional public voice alerts, can be controlled through device or app settings.

Private reporting and app review (Google Play edition)

When the private reporting service is available and you choose to submit a report, Hartmann Studios’ moderation service receives its category and reason, the relevant message or output reference and peer’s public key when applicable, your optional note, and the text excerpt only if you explicitly include it. It does not attach the conversation, voice recording, image or file, or publish the report in chat. The report uses HTTPS. Network information is used for abuse protection; the source address is not written into the moderation inbox. Reports and any recorded disposition are accessible only to authorized moderators. The reporting app can still block peers offline, but report delivery requires internet. Resolved reports are eligible for removal 90 days after the case closes; unresolved reports can be kept for review.

A separate sign-in is provided only to Google Play’s assigned app reviewers when reviewer access is enabled. The app sends their demo credentials and a fresh session request over HTTPS to validate a limited, temporary preview. Review audio is recorded and played back on that device, not sent to the mesh or the reviewer service. This is separate from Play purchases, app subscriptions and other users’ content.

On-device AI

The model is optional. Downloading it connects to its download host (Hugging Face), which receives ordinary connection information. The model runs locally. Prompts, selected images, AI answers and saved AI chats remain on the device unless you deliberately share them or include an excerpt in a private report. You can delete local AI conversations in the app. There is no guarantee that AI answers are accurate or appropriate; flag a concerning answer in the app.

Payments, advertising and optional diagnostics

On Google Play, purchase and subscription options are shown and handled by Google Play Billing. MeshTalk reads product details and purchase status to provide and restore access. MeshTalk does not receive your payment card number. The separate direct-download edition may instead use the publisher’s payment service and Stripe to handle checkout and validate access; it does not restore Play ownership.

In the Google Play edition, Google User Messaging Platform handles advertising privacy choices, and Google AdMob can provide ads for users who select the adult band, are confirmed not to have paid access and have a current ads permission state. A missing choice or an uncertain purchase check does not enable ads. Google’s services may process identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device/app information for purposes including ads, fraud prevention and measurement. Options depend on your region and settings. Use MeshTalk’s advertising privacy controls and Google’s partner privacy information to learn more.

In supporting builds, Settings → Usage & crash reporting controls optional Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring. These may handle interaction events, installation/device identifiers, crash details, and performance measurements, as well as app, OS and device information. The setting for a new installation of this Play update is off until enabled. Previously saved choices can remain. You can turn it off in Settings; this does not delete data previously sent.

Public issue feedback is separate

If you post in the optional public GitHub issue tracker, your feedback text, any contact information you enter and any attachments you select may be visible to anyone. Voluntary diagnostics can include your app version, device/Android info, locale and time zone, and storage or memory totals. Do not put sensitive details there. For abuse, use private in-app reporting where available, or contact Hartmann Studios privately using the link below; do not post a private message in the public issue tracker.

Age, control and questions

The Google Play edition is intended for ages 16 and up. The advertising band is stored locally in the app. Declining to select the adult band does not prevent messaging or purchasing; ads remain off.

Many details are stored only on your device. Clearing its app data or removing the app will remove that installation’s local copy; backups, other people’s copies, publicly shared issues and prior service records are separate. Play manages subscriptions and Play purchase records. We cannot remove copies held by another device or restore local conversations from a central server.

For privacy questions or a request concerning data we hold, use the Hartmann Studios contact page. Include a private report reference if relevant, but never send a password, private key or unnecessary message content. This is not a promise that copies controlled by other people or services can be deleted by MeshTalk.
