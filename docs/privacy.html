Privacy Policy

MeshTalk for Android · Hartmann Studios

Version notice: The descriptions of changed features below apply to MeshTalk for Google Play version 1.0.20261121.1569 and later versions with those features, when available. Opening this policy does not install or enable an update. If you use an older version or a separately distributed build, its features may differ. Read the previous policy.

At a glance: This Google Play version sends direct chat, attachments and paid Talk audio over Bluetooth between contacts who have both added each other. There is no public mesh chat or public Talk in this version. MeshTalk does not host your chat history. Internet features such as optional safety feedback, maps, Play Billing, ads, optional diagnostics and model downloads have separate data paths.

On your device and over the mesh

Your nickname, mesh identity keypair, contacts, saved messages and attachments, block list and settings are kept in app storage. The private keys used to sign and encrypt traffic stay in app storage. Your contact code and connections to accepted contacts use public keys and your chosen nickname. Android backup may copy eligible app data depending on your OS settings. Reviewer sessions are excluded from backup.

Two nearby users must both add each other’s contact codes before exchanging messages. Direct content is encrypted for the intended recipient; transport metadata such as public keys is not the same as message content. An intended recipient can keep or copy what you send. Hiding a message or blocking a contact takes effect on your device; it does not erase another person’s copy or impose a network-wide block. Older and separately distributed editions may have public mesh features.

Nearby-device and Bluetooth permissions support mesh connections. Some older Android releases require location permission for discovery. Optional location sharing puts your selected coordinates in the message. In the Play edition, you can choose whether to load a Bluetooth Radar backdrop and whether to attach a map when sharing your location. If you choose a map and the tiles are not cached, the OpenStreetMap tile service receives HTTPS requests corresponding to the area (which can disclose a small area) and ordinary network information; sharing coordinates without a map does not request tiles. The direct edition may fetch the map automatically when you use those features. Voice recording uses the microphone when you record; chat may also use media you pick. Notifications, including direct Talk alerts, can be controlled through device or app settings.

Optional safety feedback and app review (Google Play edition)

If you choose to send safety feedback over the internet, the service receives the selected feedback kind and reason and a request-specific receipt identifier for deduplication. It records when the signal was received. No message text or excerpts, captions, report notes, conversation or item references, peer keys, audio, images, files, or location are sent with the signal. The host can process ordinary connection data for abuse protection; source IP addresses are not stored in the feedback table. Signals are sent over HTTPS and are eligible for automatic deletion after seven days, with cleanup scheduled daily. Hartmann Studios can see grouped counts of recent signals, not a case inbox. Feedback is not a request for emergency help or a guarantee that another user or a specific item will be investigated. You can hide content and block a contact on your device without sending feedback.

A separate, temporary sign-in lets assigned Google Play reviewers access paid Talk without purchasing. Their demo credentials and a session request are sent over HTTPS to validate access. In this release, reviewers use two nearby phones with mutual contacts to try the same direct Bluetooth audio path. The reviewer sign-in service does not receive that audio. This does not alter consumer purchases, subscription terms, or the contact requirements.

On-device AI

The model is optional. Downloading it connects to its download host (Hugging Face), which receives ordinary connection information. The model runs locally. Prompts, selected images, answers and saved AI chats stay on the device unless you deliberately share them. In this Google Play release, optional safety feedback sends only the metadata described above and no AI conversation text or images. You can delete local AI conversations in the app. AI answers are not guaranteed to be accurate or appropriate.

Payments, advertising and optional diagnostics

On Google Play, purchase and subscription options are shown and handled by Google Play Billing. MeshTalk reads product details and purchase status to provide and restore access. MeshTalk does not receive your payment card number. The separate direct-download edition may instead use the publisher’s payment service and Stripe to handle checkout and validate access; it does not restore Play ownership.

In the Google Play edition, Google User Messaging Platform handles advertising privacy choices, and Google AdMob can provide ads for users who select the adult band, are confirmed not to have paid access and have a current ads permission state. A missing choice or an uncertain purchase check does not enable ads. Google’s services may process identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device/app information for purposes including ads, fraud prevention and measurement. Options depend on your region and settings. Use MeshTalk’s advertising privacy controls and Google’s partner privacy information to learn more.

In supporting builds, Settings → Usage & crash reporting controls optional Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring. These may handle interaction events, installation/device identifiers, crash details, and performance measurements, as well as app, OS and device information. The setting for a new installation of this Play update is off until enabled. Previously saved choices can remain. You can turn it off in Settings; this does not delete data previously sent.

Public issue feedback is separate

If you independently post in the public GitHub issue tracker, your feedback text, any contact information you enter and any attachments you select may be visible to anyone. Voluntary diagnostics can include your app version, device/Android info, locale and time zone, and storage or memory totals. Do not put sensitive details there. For abuse, use the app’s local hide and block controls, send optional safety feedback, or contact Hartmann Studios privately using the link below; do not post a private message in the public issue tracker.

Age, control and questions

The Google Play edition is intended for ages 16 and up. The advertising band is stored locally in the app. Declining to select the adult band does not prevent messaging or purchasing; ads remain off.

Many details are stored only on your device. Clearing its app data or removing the app will remove that installation’s local copy; backups, other people’s copies, publicly shared issues and prior service records are separate. Play manages subscriptions and Play purchase records. We cannot remove copies held by another device or restore local conversations from a central server.

For privacy questions or a request concerning data we hold, use the Hartmann Studios contact page. Include a feedback receipt identifier only if relevant. Never send a password, private key or unnecessary message content. This is not a promise that copies controlled by other people or services can be deleted by MeshTalk.
